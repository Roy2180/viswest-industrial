ABOUT VISWEST

A practical industrial company with a people-first foundation.

VisWest Industrial Services is being developed from hands-on experience in mechanical maintenance and shutdown work. We understand what clients expect from dependable tradespeople: safety, reliability, practical knowledge, teamwork and quality execution.

Our roots are in the Filipino skilled-trades community in Australia, while our future workforce will be open to qualified people from every background. Recruitment and employment decisions will be based on skills, qualifications, work rights, safety attitude and suitability for each role.

PROPOSED SERVICES

Mechanical trades for maintenance, shutdowns and projects.

Our initial service is intended to be specialist labour supply to established recruitment, fabrication, maintenance and industrial contractors.

01

Mechanical Fitters

Experienced personnel for fixed-plant maintenance, materials handling and shutdown execution.

  • Conveyors & pulleys
  • Gearboxes & couplings
  • Bearings & drive systems
  • Hydraulics & lubrication
  • Crushers & screens

02

Boilermakers & Fabricators

Trades support for structural repairs, fabrication and plant maintenance.

  • Chutes & liners
  • Structural repairs
  • Welding & fabrication
  • Shutdown maintenance

03

Dual-Trade Personnel

Multi-skilled tradespeople supporting complex industrial maintenance and troubleshooting environments.

04

Shutdown Crews

Small, experienced crews for planned shutdowns, maintenance campaigns and project support.
