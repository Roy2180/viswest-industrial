OUR PEOPLE

Filipino roots. Australian standards. Open to skilled people from every background.

VisWest has roots in the Filipino skilled-trades community in Australia. We want to bring experienced tradespeople together, support one another and connect strong trade capability with Australian industry expectations.

Employment opportunities will be based on qualifications, competency, work rights, safety attitude, experience and suitability for the role.
